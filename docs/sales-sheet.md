# Confirmi: Sales Sheet

## 1. What you are selling (in one line)

> **Confirmi turns your orders into delivered cash, with fewer fake orders and fewer returns, and shows the seller their real profit.**

*Careful: Confirmi does not track ad clicks. It starts at the order form. Click and cost-per-click data stay in Meta / TikTok Ads Manager; ad spend is typed in by hand to calculate profit.*

Darija: *"Rak tkhalles l'pub 3la kol commande. Confirmi y'khalli ktar mn les commandes ywelliw drahem f yeddek, w ywerrik l'bénéfice l'7a9i9i."*

**Who it's for:** Algerian online sellers who run Facebook / Instagram / TikTok ads and get paid **cash on delivery (COD)**.

**What it is:** their own private dashboard (web app, works on phone and laptop, Arabic + French). One store, one login, their data only.

---

## 2. The problem (what the seller lives every day)

With COD, **an order is not money yet.** Many orders never become cash:

| Pain | What it costs them |
|---|---|
| **Fake / joke / duplicate orders** | Time wasted calling, parcels shipped for nothing |
| **No answer** | Orders die because nobody follows up |
| **Returns** (customer refuses at the door) | Delivery paid both ways + the ad money for that order is lost + stock stuck for days |
| **Chaos** (Messenger, Excel, notebook, WhatsApp) | Nobody knows who was called, who said "call me tomorrow", who refused before |
| **Fake profit** | "200 orders today!" but no idea what they actually earned after ads, delivery and returns |

---

## 3. How Confirmi solves it

| Pain | Confirmi feature |
|---|---|
| Fake / duplicate orders | Order form with Algerian phone check, duplicate warning, spam protection |
| Losing track of calls | **"To confirm" queue**: oldest first, one-tap result (Confirmed / No answer / Call back / Cancelled), call-back reminders, call count |
| Bad customers | Every phone number has a **history** + **risk badge** + **blacklist**. You see the refuser *before* shipping |
| Slow confirming | One tap to call, one tap to send a ready **WhatsApp** message |
| Shipping hassle | **ZR Express**: send in bulk, print labels, statuses update; Excel export for other couriers |
| Missing new orders | **Telegram alert** for every order + evening summary |
| Ads not learning | **Meta & TikTok Pixel** events on the order page |
| Orders from chat | **Auto-reply message** for Messenger / Instagram / WhatsApp with order links |
| "Am I making money?" | **Real profit** = delivered sales − product cost − delivery − returns − ad spend |

---

## 4. Quick app tour (what to show)

1. **Order form:** the link the seller puts in ads. The customer picks wilaya, commune, home or stop desk; delivery fee is added automatically.
2. **To confirm:** the call list. Tap call → tap the result. Done.
3. **Customers:** phone history, risk badge, blacklist.
4. **Delivery:** send confirmed orders to the courier, track Delivered / Returned.
5. **Stats:** confirmation rate, delivery rate, return rate, **real profit**, charts.
6. **Settings:** products, delivery fees per wilaya, WhatsApp message, pixels, Telegram, auto-reply.

**Demo rule:** show only 3 things, on your phone, in under 3 minutes:
**order form → confirm queue → risky customer badge + profit screen.**

## 5. How to run the conversation

### Step 1: Ask, don't present
- How many orders a day?
- What % do you confirm? What % come back as returns?
- Who confirms, and how? (Excel? notebook?)
- Do you know your real profit this month?

### Step 2: Do the math with them (use *their* numbers)
Example:
- 30 orders/day ≈ **900/month**
- 25% returned = **225 returns**
- Each return ≈ 400–800 DZD delivery + the ad cost of that order
- ≈ **150,000+ DZD lost per month**

> "If Confirmi cuts your returns by just a few points, it pays for itself."

*(These figures are examples. Always replace them with the client's real numbers.)*

## 6. Objections and answers

| They say | You answer |
|---|---|
| "Excel is fine." | "Excel doesn't warn you this number refused 2 parcels last month, or remind you to call back at 6pm." |
| "Too expensive." | "Compare it to **one month of returns**. That's the real price." |
| "I'm not technical." | "I set it all up. You only tap buttons, on your phone." |
| "What about my data?" | "It's **your own install**. Your customers, your data, shared with nobody." |
| "Can it take orders from Messenger?" | "Yes: an auto-reply sends your order links, the customer fills the form, the order lands in Confirmi." |
